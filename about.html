Our
Story

WITHX Innovations Private Limited was founded by Sharad Ashruba Waybase to build indigenous unmanned defence hardware for problems the industry hadn't solved: how to extend surveillance beyond the limits of drone battery endurance, and how to prevent the permanent loss of UAVs after an accidental water impact.

Registered as WITHX Innovations Private Limited (CIN U71100PN2026PTC252377) and headquartered at Dehu Phata, Alandi (D), Taluka-Khed, in the Pune district of Maharashtra, the company operates as an engineering-led product house — taking each system from CAD and rapid prototyping through to working hardware.

WITHX is recognised as a startup under DPIIT, with patent drafting currently in progress on its core recovery technology. The company's work is designed and developed under the Make in India initiative, aimed at delivering scalable, modular, cost-effective capability for defence, homeland security, and disaster response.
